Lately, I have been working on the design for a mobile visual novel game called "New Love".

It will be released in early 2025. I will post the link here when it's done.

I started with a UX prototype, then converted it to a high-fidelity mockup using Figma, as always.

I proposed an [animated flow](https://vimeo.com/1044607188) that serves as guidance for developers and demonstrates micro-interactions.

The app is locked in landscape mode. The main screen follows a chat pattern with speech dialogs.
